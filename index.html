High Speed PRNG for Homomorphic Encryption

IEEE ISCAIE 2025

Published paper, second author. An FPGA pseudo-random number generator combining a ChaCha6 core with a 32-bit LFSR, written in Verilog in Xilinx Vivado. It reached 2.78 Gbps and passed all 15 NIST statistical tests. I wrote the LFSR and the ChaCha quarter-round logic, and ran the NIST test suite.

Verilog · Xilinx Vivado · FPGA · NIST SP 800-22
